SOLMAR HEAT

Open-world crime in the fictional coastal city of Solmar. Original game — no Rockstar / GTA assets.

How to play: WASD or arrow keys drive / walk, Shift run, Space enter or exit a car, Enter start the next story mission, P toggle autopilot (demo driver).
Goal: finish all 8 story missions for Rico & Dante — deliveries, getaways, a heist — while the Solmar police chase you. Wanted stars rise when you steal cars or hit pedestrians, and fall when you stay out of sight. Get busted on foot or wasted and you lose money and the mission.
